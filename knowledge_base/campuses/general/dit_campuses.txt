Dar es Salaam Institute of Technology (DIT) currently operates four campuses.

The Main Campus is located in Dar es Salaam.

DIT also operates Mwanza Campus in Mwanza Region, Songwe Campus in Songwe Region, and Dodoma Campus in Dodoma Region.

DIT provides technical education in applied science, engineering, technology and related professional fields.
