Dar es Salaam Institute of Technology currently has four campuses.

The campuses are:

1. Main Campus in Dar es Salaam.

2. Mwanza Campus in Mwanza Region.

3. Songwe Campus in Songwe Region.

4. Dodoma Campus in Dodoma Region, also referred to as Dodoma Technical College or DTC-DIT.

DIT offers technical education and professional training in applied science, engineering, technology and related fields.

Programme availability differs between campuses.

When answering questions about a specific campus, the chatbot should use campus-specific information where available and should not assume that every DIT programme is offered at every campus.
