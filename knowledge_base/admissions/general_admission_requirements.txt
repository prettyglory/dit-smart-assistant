ORDINARY DIPLOMA

For direct admission into Ordinary Diploma programmes, an applicant normally needs a Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE) with at least four passes at D grade or higher.

For many engineering and technology programmes, the required subjects include Physics or Engineering Science, Mathematics, Chemistry, and another eligible subject.

Some programmes have programme-specific requirements.

Applicants with National Vocational Award Level III or Trade Test Grade I qualifications may also be eligible for relevant programmes, subject to the applicable DIT admission requirements.

BACHELOR DEGREE - THREE YEAR PROGRAMMES

For three-year Bachelor of Engineering or Bachelor of Technology programmes, applicants may qualify with a relevant Ordinary Diploma, NTA Level 6, or an equivalent qualification.

The relevant Ordinary Diploma should normally have a minimum GPA of 3.0.

Applicants must also satisfy the applicable secondary education requirements.

Applicants with a relevant Full Technician Certificate (FTC) or equivalent qualification may also be considered according to DIT admission regulations.

BACHELOR DEGREE - FOUR YEAR PROGRAMMES

Applicants using Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (ACSEE) qualifications may qualify through relevant science combinations.

Accepted combinations include:

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM).

Physics, Geography and Mathematics (PGM).

Applicants must have principal passes in Mathematics and Physics from the same sitting.

The total points should meet the minimum points required by DIT for the applicable admission year.

IMPORTANT

Admission requirements can vary by programme and may change between academic years.

Applicants should always confirm current programme-specific requirements using the official DIT admission portal before applying.
