DIT ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Admission requirements depend on the academic level and programme for which a student is applying.

ORDINARY DIPLOMA - NTA LEVEL 4 TO 6

General direct-entry requirement:

An applicant should hold a Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE) with at least four passes at D grade or higher.

For most engineering and technology Ordinary Diploma programmes, the passes should include:

Physics or Engineering Science.
Mathematics.
Chemistry.
One other subject excluding a religious subject.

Therefore, the minimum general pass grade is D grade or higher in the required subjects.

For Science and Laboratory Technology, Biomedical Equipment Engineering, Food Science and Technology, Leather Products Technology, Leather Processing Technology, Food Processing Technology and Biotechnology, applicants should normally have D grade or higher in:

Physics or Engineering Science.
Mathematics.
Chemistry.
Biology.

For Information Technology and Communication System Technology, applicants should normally have D grade or higher in:

Physics or Engineering Science.
Mathematics.
Any other two subjects excluding a religious subject.

Alternative entry may also be available to applicants holding:

General Certificate Course in Engineering (GCE).

OR

National Vocational Award (NVA) Level III or Trade Test Certificate Grade I in the relevant field, together with at least D grade in Mathematics at CSEE.


BACHELOR DEGREE - THREE YEAR PROGRAMMES

This route applies mainly to applicants who already hold a relevant Ordinary Diploma, NTA Level 6, or equivalent qualification.

An applicant with a relevant Ordinary Diploma should have:

A relevant Ordinary Diploma, NTA Level 6 or equivalent.

Minimum GPA of 3.0.

The qualification should be from a recognized institution.

The applicant should also satisfy the relevant CSEE subject requirements.

For applicants with a Full Technician Certificate (FTC):

The qualification should be in a relevant field.

The applicant should have at least an average grade of C.

Alternatively, the applicant should have an average of at least 3 points using the scale:

A = 5.
B = 4.
C = 3.
D = 2.

Relevant secondary-school subject requirements must also be satisfied.


BACHELOR DEGREE - FOUR YEAR PROGRAMMES

This route applies mainly to applicants using Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (ACSEE) qualifications.

Accepted science combinations include:

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM).

Physics, Geography and Mathematics (PGM).

Applicants should have principal passes in Mathematics and Physics from the same sitting.

The total points should be at least 4.0 according to the applicable DIT admission conversion scale.

Applicants with an Ordinary Diploma, NTA Level 6, or Full Technician Certificate who wish to change their field of study may also be considered subject to the applicable DIT requirements.


IMPORTANT

The minimum requirements above are general admission requirements.

Individual programmes may have additional or programme-specific requirements.

Admission requirements and cut-off conditions may change between academic years.

Applicants should always confirm the current requirements through the official DIT admission portal before submitting an application.
